    Participants will discuss the potential impacts of genAI chatbot use on opportunities for guided practice in a workshop, how expertise affects an individual’s ability to make successful use of genAI tools, how considerations of cognitive load should change in this new context, and how to maintain a positive learning environment in their workshops.     Jon's teaching demo template: 

    ============

    SESSION INFO

    ============

    ==============

    SPIEL

    ==============


    1. Hello and welcome

    2. Code of Conduct

    3. Quick intros - name and organization

    4. Not a high stakes test! Sometimes we ask people to repeat - provide feedback when we do this, doesn't mean we don't want you. We do! But we want to make sure you'll have the best experience when you start teaching.

    5. Process:

        1. Present in random order

        1. When it's your turn, I will let you know.

        1. Take a moment as needed to get set up - run code, create variables, set up environment, etc.

        1. Let me know when ready, share screen and I will give a 3-2-1 countdown

        1. Present 5 minutes from your episode, beginning with the beginning.

        1. I will keep time, everyone else please make sure you're muted.

        1. After 5 minutes I will let you know. 

        1. Feedback - begin with yourself. Use 2x2 framework we used in instructor training.

        1. As time allows we will have feedback from the group. Can also add feedback to the etherpad.

        1. Etc. until everyone has had a chance to present their demo.

    6. As time allows I can answer any additional questions you have.

    7. I won't be able to tell you today if you completed this checkout step - will follow up by email by the end of the day Thursday.

    8. What questions do you have?
